# Policy audit

Does the site have policy pages?
1. Is there a last updated date for each policy?
    - Having an up to date privacy policy is important, as privacy laws change, and new ones go into effect, requiring new disclosures over time
2. Do they have a Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Disclaimer and Cookie Policy page?
    - Are these pages clearly hyperlinked and accessible in the footer?
    - Are the policy pages their own links or are they combined? Best to be separate (see article: https://termageddon.com/consolidating-policies-one-page/)
3. Does the customer know what laws they created their Privacy Policy to cover?
